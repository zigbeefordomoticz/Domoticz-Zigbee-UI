The aim of the plugin is to bridge a ZigBee coordinator to the Domoticz Home Automation in order to communicate with objects using the ZigBee protocol.

For information about the Zigbee for DomoticZ Plugin, please refer to the Plugin Wiki.

How to deal with issues and questions

  • If you have a question or a problem, please ask on the Domoticz Forum.

    You can use the English Domoticz forum.

Come and discuss with us!

We are a small team of enthusiasts who share the pleasure of using DomoticZ with ZigBee coordinators.

Meet us on keybase.io for real time discussions.
